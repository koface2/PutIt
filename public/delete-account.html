Delete Your PutIt Account

You can permanently delete your PutIt account and all associated data directly from within the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the PutIt app and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile icon in the top-right corner to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account & Data".
  4. Confirm the deletion. Your account and all stored items will be permanently removed.

What data is deleted

  • Your account credentials (email / Google account link)
  • All items you have saved to PutIt
  • Your household settings and sync data

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Need help?

If you are unable to access the app, contact us at koface@gmail.com and we will manually delete your data within 30 days.
